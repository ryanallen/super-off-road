# Super Off-Road NES remake

Play at [ryanallen.com/super-off-road](https://ryanallen.com/super-off-road/). For local play, run `python -m http.server 8077 --bind 127.0.0.1` in this folder and open `http://127.0.0.1:8077/`.

The browser build uses captures of all eight original NES courses and race HUDs, numbered pickup sprites, the qualifying screen, Speed Shop and ending art from the [99-race NES recording](https://tasvideos.org/5294M), the [original title image](https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php?title=Ivan_%22Ironman%22_Stewart%27s_Super_Off_Road_%28NES%29), and [NES menu and podium captures](https://classicgamesdb.com/platform/nes/ivan-ironman-stewart-s-super-off-road/). The race order, four-lap rule, shop prices and limits, prizes and lives follow the [NES manual](https://www.digitpress.com/library/manuals/nes/Ivan%20%27Iron%20Man%27%20Stewart%27s%20Super%20Off-Road.pdf) and the [documented race sequence](https://www.speedrun.com/ivan_ironman_stewarts_super_off_road_nes/forums/la3l1). Original captures and music live in `assets/`. The full recording is not part of this project.

On an Xbox controller, use the **D-pad or left stick** to steer and choose menu items, **A** to accelerate or select, **B** for nitro or name backspace, and **Start/Menu** to pause or finish a name. On the name screen, choose letters with the D-pad or stick and add them with A; select **FINISH** with A or press Start/Menu to accept the name. Up to four controllers can race. On a keyboard, player one uses **Left/Right**, **X**, **Z**, and **Enter**; player two uses **A/D**, **J**, and **K**. The menus choose one to four players, names and countries.

Run `python fetch-audio.py` to retrieve the [original NES music recordings](https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php?title=Ivan_%22Ironman%22_Stewart%27s_Super_Off_Road_%28NES%29) into `assets/music/`. The title, start, track, finish and national anthem music play in the browser.

Trucks now skid, slow in mud and water, hop over ramp art, rebound from collisions, and gain grip or bounce control from tire and shock upgrades. CPU trucks use the same steering and collision code as players. Numbered pickups use sprites extracted from the NES recording and appear at about the recorded interval; Fandango uses observed positions. Engine, nitro, collision and pickup cues are synthesized in the browser to resemble NES sounds; isolated original effect samples are still needed for an exact audio match. Exact handling and pickup rules, four-player controls and the continue cutoff still need comparison with an original cartridge. [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) lists the original sources and remaining verification work.
